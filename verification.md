## Pre-flight audit (HARD GATE - Section 10) - PASSED, findings resolved as follows

Full output: data/audit_output.txt (script: code/audit_section10.py). Findings and dispositions:

1. **Council_Dist spelling mix (the audit's central trap):** the string field mixes zero-padded ("05") and bare ("5") spellings; 776 rows use the bare spelling. Read naively, District 2 looks like 5,593 and a phantom one-digit district set appears. All district figures use the integer-normalized district; after normalization exactly 35 districts appear. Blank or "None" on 649 rows (0.6 percent), counted in totals and excluded from district shares.
2. **Contract_Value behaves as the parent project value:** among records with a positive stated value on a real parcel, 19,625 repeat a (parcel, stated value) pair carried by another trade permit on the same parcel; one District 25 project carries the same $53,809,905 statement on at least six electrical permits, and a $67,865,689 statement sits on an electrical service release (a connection event, not the project itself). The layer's metadata does not define the field's basis, so the brief describes the behavior and asks Metro to define it; it does not assert the official definition. Contract_Value is never summed anywhere in the brief; the only aggregate value figure is the deduplicated-by-parcel count of parcels carrying a stated value of $10 million or more (141 parcels, 117 in District 19). Medians use the positive-value population only (104,497 records); 6,822 records state zero and 2 state none. Round values are common (8.2 percent of positive values are exactly $5,000) and that is printed in the brief.
3. **Dates are true timestamps, all present for entry:** Date_Entered is populated on all 111,321 records; Date_Issued on 107,213. No placeholder-midnight pattern (UTC hour census spans the working day) and no pre-1990 sentinel dates in either field. Entry dates in the snapshot end October 16, 2026; the brief states that end date wherever a period is named and never describes the register past it.
4. **Negative and zero gaps:** 3 records have an issued date before the entered date (printed in the audit output with permit numbers) and are excluded from gap figures; same-day zero gaps (93,345, computed on America/Chicago local dates) are real issuance events at this counter, retained. NOTE (found by the adversarial review, fixed before publication): the audit's first run computed gaps on UTC calendar dates and counted 93,357 zero gaps; the 12-permit difference is records crossing Chicago midnight. code/audit_section10.py now converts to America/Chicago dates, the audit output recorded in data/audit_output.txt is the corrected run, and its LAG POLARITY line reads zero=93,345, matching the brief.
5. **Status and issued-date cross-tab:** REJECTED (674), OPEN (13), and nearly all OK TO PAY (298 of 299) records carry no issued date; DONE records almost all carry one (81,660 of 81,662). Because the layer does not define its statuses, the brief reports the cross-tab as recorded and does not treat Date_Issued presence as a status definition.
6. **Parcel sentinel:** 626 records carry Parcel = "RIGHT-OF-WAY"; excluded from the parcel join and parcel counts, and the exclusion is printed in the brief. Parcel is populated on every record (no blanks). PermitNumber is unique on 111,273 records; 48 numbers appear on more than one record, consistent with multi-row cases; OBJECTID is unique on all rows.
7. **Address field:** blank on 46,629 records (41.9 percent); no address-level claim is made anywhere in the brief.
8. **Units:** every share in the brief is permits / permits inside this register (or parcels / parcels in the join, labeled), or a median over the stated positive-value population. Nothing mixes permits with dollars except the labeled parcel footprint (distinct parcels carrying a stated value at or above a printed threshold).
9. **Derived figures labeled:** the 3.8 trade-permits-per-building-permit ratio and the parcel match rates are labeled derived, with both windows printed. Percentages written as "X of Y" use the printed population (dated records for gap shares, district-known records for district shares, value-positive records for value medians).

## Adversarial review (Standards Section 11)

A fresh reviewer who did not build the package re-derived every headline figure from the raw snapshot (pandas, independent code path; parcel join recomputed against the stack parquet) and attacked the framing. **All eight numbered headline claims reproduced exactly as counts, shares, medians, and joins** (rows 111,321; trade split; same-day 93,345 and 87.1 percent; service family 22,701 and 43.2 percent; District 19 at 6,565 and 5.9 percent; 35 districts; median district 3,037; megaproject parcels 141 with 117 in District 19; join 75,463 of 110,695 = 68.2 percent and 15,341 shared parcels; value repeats 19,625 under the audit's stated population; building median 29 days re-derived from the companion layer itself; beer 22.2 percent and 11.4x from the beer package's sources). The reviewer then found the following defects, each adjudicated here. NO FINDING IS LEFT OPEN.

**Finding A (BLOCKER) - "every other common kind clears at 89 percent or better" was false.** The draft's slow-lane paragraph generalized from four kinds; the package's own subtype table shows outage and reconnect permits at 71.6 percent (4,728 dated), temporary service at 88.5 percent (3,021 dated), service reconnects at 62.9 percent (669 dated), and the small Btu classes at 86.6 to 88.8 percent. DISPOSITION: ACCEPTED AND FIXED. The paragraph now names the true slow lanes (change-outs 46.0 percent; service reconnects 62.9 percent; outage and reconnect 71.6 percent), bounds the rest ("every other kind with at least 1,000 dated permits clears between 86.6 and 99.5 percent"), and adds the slowest tiny classes (photovoltaic, 143 dated permits, median 13 to 15 days). stats.json now carries the full 18-subtype table, and data/subtype_panel.csv carries a permits_dated_nonneg column. The repeated error in "Who can act" was fixed to "89 percent or better for each of the register's four largest permit kinds" (92.6 / 89.9 / 89.2 / 95.1 percent, all verified).

**Finding B (BLOCKER) - the "existing city, not new wiring" characterization of the service family was unsupported and contradicted.** Service releases (14,064) are routinely the energizing step for new construction; the reviewer found 45.6 percent of their Purpose statements contain "NEW". DISPOSITION: ACCEPTED AND FIXED, with the characterization replaced by measured evidence computed for the adjudication (data/service_family_detail.json): service releases sit on a building-permit parcel 76.1 percent of the time (full electrical permits: 78.9 percent), while emergency reconnects sit on one 9.4 percent, outage and reconnect 25.6 percent, service reconnects 35.5 percent. The brief now presents the family as service-connection events that split by evidence (releases track construction; reconnects mostly serve addresses with no building permit in the window), and the thesis was revised a second time (recorded in reviewer-notes.md). The README and social copy were corrected to match.

**Finding C (SHOULD-FIX) - headline denominator slippage.** The one-line summary, hero, README, and social copy framed 87.1 percent against all 111,321 entered permits; the true all-entered share is 83.9 percent (93,345 of 111,321). DISPOSITION: ACCEPTED AND FIXED. Every surface now names the dated population (summary: "107,213 carry an issuance date, and 87.1 percent of those"; hero: "of Nashville's dated trade permits"; social: "87.1% of Nashville's dated trade permits (93,345 of 107,210)"). The brief body's first bullet already carried the correct denominators.

**Finding D (SHOULD-FIX) - audit and brief on different clocks.** The audit's first run used UTC calendar dates (93,357 zero gaps) against the brief's America/Chicago basis (93,345). DISPOSITION: ACCEPTED AND FIXED. code/audit_section10.py converts to America/Chicago dates with the discrepancy documented in a comment; the recorded audit output is the corrected run (zero=93,345).

**Finding E (SHOULD-FIX) - Contract_Value semantics asserted in one place, disclaimed in two others.** DISPOSITION: ACCEPTED AND FIXED. The brief now states that the metadata does not define the field's basis and that the records behave as the parent project's value; the "Who can act" ask for a definition is therefore consistent, and the 19,625 figure carries its population (positive values on real parcels) in the brief text.

**Finding F (SHOULD-FIX) - verdict language outside "What this might mean".** Instances: "the fastest and flattest of the three" (the pending register has no issuance speed), "the work that follows every building project", the section heading "half service work" for 43.2 percent, "does not pile up the way this series' other registers do". DISPOSITION: ACCEPTED AND FIXED. Each was replaced with a measured statement or removed ("every" deleted; heading now "two in five electrical permits are service-connection events"; the district section opens "Trade permit volume spreads across the whole county"; the opening now reads "Its permits clear faster than the building register's, and no district holds even 6 percent of them").

**Finding G (SHOULD-FIX) - the published snapshot republished applicant names.** The raw CSV's Contact column contradicts the brief's "names are never published" line. DISPOSITION: ACCEPTED AND FIXED. The Contact column is withheld from the package's data/trade_permits_raw.csv (all 111,321 rows and every other column retained; audit, stats, and verifier re-run on the redacted file and still pass, verifier 27/27). The full record is retained only in the internal warehouse snapshot parquet. The brief's methods note now says exactly this.

**Finding H (SHOULD-FIX) - building-layer window label.** stats.json and sources.md described the companion layer as "snapshot 2026-10-30" while its issues run to November 2, 2026. DISPOSITION: ACCEPTED AND FIXED in this package: the layer is now described as "from the Machine Readable brief of 2026-11-03 (issue dates 2023-10-30 to 2026-11-02)" in the brief, sources.md, and stats.json. The label in the sibling package is the sibling's to correct; a follow-up row is recommended.

**Nits (all accepted and fixed):** "1,100 days" is the date difference; the inclusive span is 1,101 days (brief and stats.json corrected). The 1,114 permits carrying $10M+ includes one RIGHT-OF-WAY record; the brief now uses the real-parcel figure 1,113 and stats.json carries both. data/subtype_panel.csv mixed totals with dated shares (dated-n column added). The beer contrast now prints its population difference and the dated-only sensitivity in the brief. "By ZIP code, the leaders are residential" carried no source (now "sit away from downtown").
