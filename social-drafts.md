## Held copy - lead with the killer number

87.1% of Nashville's dated trade permits were issued the same day they were entered (93,345 of 107,210).

111,321 trade permits in all, October 2023 to October 2026. The median building permit in the companion register waited 29 days. Trade work does not pile up downtown either: no council district holds even 6% of it, and 43% of the electrical permits are connection events (service releases and reconnects), not wiring permits.

The one slow lane: HVAC unit change-outs, same-day only 46% of the time.

Full brief, data, and audit: https://github.com/adowlen/nashville-trade-permits-data-brief

## Variant (district angle)

Every district, same speed. Nashville's 111,321 trade permits spread across all 35 council districts - the largest holds 5.9% - and 87.1% of dated permits issued the day they were entered. The big projects still cluster: 117 of the 141 parcels with a trade permit stating $10M+ in project value are in District 19.
